How verification works

Evidence becomes a bounded decision.

QuorumX keeps discovery convenient while moving the trust-critical assessment into an inspectable consensus process.

  1. 01

    Index the source

    The public proposal, author, choices and voting window are normalized from its canonical Snapshot space.

  2. 02

    Fix the evidence

    Independent validators retrieve the same source and establish agreement over its normalized material.

  3. 03

    Investigate material findings

    The contract extracts source-cited material facts; validators independently retrieve the source and require an exact canonical match.

  4. 04

    Publish the proof

    An accepted assessment exposes its content hash, transaction and provenance for anyone to inspect.

What “accepted” means

Validators reached consensus over a source-backed assessment. It does not mean the proposal is objectively unsafe, rejected by its DAO, or blocked by QuorumX.

About QuorumX

An independent review layer for collective decisions.

QuorumX helps delegates, security reviewers, treasury teams and the public see which governance decisions deserve closer attention—and why.

It never votes, executes proposals or replaces human judgment. Public browsing remains open; a wallet is needed only for future participation features.
